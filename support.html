Camera access

Camera permission is requested when you choose to scan. If access was denied, open iOS Settings → QR Code Limitless → Camera. You can also use manual entry or image scanning where available.

Restore Premium

Open QR Code Limitless → Settings → Subscription → Restore purchases. App Store purchases remain tied to your Apple Account even if local app data is reset.
