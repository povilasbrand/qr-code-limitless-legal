Privacy Policy

QR Code Limitless · Effective November 23, 2026 · Contact: Give Feedback form

Who we are

QR Code Limitless (“we”, “us”) is a QR code and barcode utility. This policy explains what information the app uses and the choices available to you.

Local-first data

Scan and creation history, favourites, notes or folders, app settings, and similar library data are stored on your device. The app stores this library locally. User-directed exports and sharing can transfer data elsewhere; when local-only mode is off, an eligible product or reference scan may also trigger an optional public information lookup using its identifier.

Camera, photos, contacts and location

Camera access is used when you choose to scan. Photos selected for code scanning are decoded locally where supported and are not uploaded merely to detect a QR code or barcode. Contacts are accessed only when you choose a contact while creating a code. Precise location is requested only when you choose a location feature such as “Use current location”.

Network lookups

Core QR/barcode scanning and generation do not require our server. When local-only mode is off, an eligible product or reference scan may automatically initiate a permitted public lookup. Such requests can send the barcode, ISBN, VIN, or similar identifier and ordinary connection metadata to a third-party information service such as Open Food Facts, Open Products Facts, Open Library, or NHTSA. You can turn on local-only mode to avoid these lookups while continuing to scan and create codes offline. Shopping links open the selected third-party service. Backend-only provider integrations are disabled in this release.

Request a code type (optional)

If you choose the Request a code type form and tap Send request, we send the email address and free-text request you entered to our HTTPS request relay hosted on Cloudflare. The service uses the connecting IP address to help limit abuse and forwards the message to our support mailbox using an email-delivery service. We use these details to review and, where possible, reply to your request, not to advertise to you. These messages may remain in the delivery provider and recipient mailbox until deleted in the course of support operations; we cannot promise a fixed retention period for third-party transmission logs. To request deletion of your correspondence, contact Give Feedback form, subject to applicable legal requirements. You do not need to submit a request to use core scanning, code creation, History or exporting.

No user accounts

The current release has no user account or sign-in system. Scanning, creation, local history, Premium purchase, and Restore Purchases do not require an app account.

Purchases

Apple processes App Store payments. RevenueCat receives purchase and entitlement information and uses an app-generated anonymous identifier so the app can unlock Premium features and restore purchases. We do not create a login identity for this purpose and do not receive or store your full payment-card number.

Backups and exports

When you export or back up your library, the resulting files are created at your request and may contain scan history or created-code data. You control where those files are saved or shared. Review their contents before sharing.

Service providers

Services used by the current release may include Apple (App Store and device services), RevenueCat (subscription entitlements), Google AdMob (advertising when enabled), maps/location services when requested, and public product/reference providers such as Open Food Facts, Open Products Facts, Open Library, and NHTSA. The current release does not use Clerk or a Supabase app backend. The optional code-type request uses Cloudflare and an email-delivery provider, rather than an app-owned scan-history database.

Advertising, Google AdMob and consent

The app includes Google AdMob advertising support. When ad serving is enabled, eligible free users without an active Pro subscription or introductory free trial may see an adaptive banner immediately above the Scanner tab navigation, or a full-screen App Open advertisement on returning to the app. Active Pro and free-trial users do not receive ads. Enabling Local-only mode suppresses advertising requests in the reviewed implementation. We do not provide your decoded QR/barcode contents, local History, or saved codes as advertising-targeting parameters.

Google AdMob and its advertising partners may process technical information necessary to serve, measure, secure, and improve advertising, including an IP address, approximate location derived from network information, device or app identifiers where permitted, device/app information, diagnostics, advertisement interactions, and consent signals. The app configures ad requests as non-personalized, but that does not mean no technical, security, or measurement data is processed. For applicable regions, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) manages advertising consent choices before an ad request; where available, advertising privacy options can be revisited through Settings. See Google's Privacy Policy for more on Google's processing.

Sensitive code contents

The app does not intentionally send decoded QR or barcode contents to advertising or analytics services by default. Sensitive values such as Wi-Fi passwords are redacted from exports and summaries where the app identifies them as sensitive. Avoid scanning or sharing secrets unless you trust the destination.

Retention

Local app data remains until you delete it, reset the app, or remove the app. There is no app-managed iCloud or CloudKit synchronization; iOS device backups, including iCloud Backup when enabled by you, are a separate Apple-controlled feature. We do not maintain a server-side user-account or scan-history database for this release. Apple, RevenueCat, and optional third-party information services retain information according to their own policies and legal obligations.

Your choices

Reset local data from Settings → Privacy & Data → Reset everything & onboarding. Manage permissions in iOS Settings. Cancel Premium in Apple ID → Subscriptions. Use Restore Purchases after reinstalling. There is no app account to delete in the current release. Privacy questions can be sent to Give Feedback form.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 or the minimum age required in your country.

International users

Service providers may process data in the United States or other countries where they operate.

Changes

We may update this policy. The Effective date will change when we do.
