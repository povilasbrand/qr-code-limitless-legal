Privacy Policy

QR Code Limitless · Effective October 7, 2026 · Contact: acceptus.trust@riseup.net

Who we are

QR Code Limitless (“we”, “us”) is a QR code and barcode utility. This policy explains what information the app uses and the choices available to you.

Local-first data

Scan and creation history, favourites, notes or folders, app settings, and similar library data are stored on your device. They leave the device only when you explicitly export, back up, share, or use a specific network lookup.

Camera, photos, contacts and location

Camera access is used when you choose to scan. Photos selected for code scanning are decoded locally where supported and are not uploaded merely to detect a QR code or barcode. Contacts are accessed only when you choose a contact while creating a code. Precise location is requested only when you choose a location feature such as “Use current location”.

Network lookups

Core QR/barcode scanning and generation do not require our server. Optional public-data features may send a barcode, ISBN, VIN, or similar identifier directly to a third-party information service such as Open Food Facts, Open Products Facts, Open Library, or NHTSA. Shopping links open the selected third-party service. Backend-only provider integrations are disabled in this release.

No user accounts

The current release has no user account or sign-in system. Scanning, creation, local history, Premium purchase, and Restore Purchases do not require an app account.

Purchases

Apple processes App Store payments. RevenueCat receives purchase and entitlement information and uses an app-generated anonymous identifier so the app can unlock Premium features and restore purchases. We do not create a login identity for this purpose and do not receive or store your full payment-card number.

Backups and exports

When you export or back up your library, the resulting files are created at your request and may contain scan history or created-code data. You control where those files are saved or shared. Review their contents before sharing.

Service providers

Services used by the current release may include Apple (App Store and device services), RevenueCat (subscription entitlements), maps/location services when requested, and public product/reference providers such as Open Food Facts, Open Products Facts, Open Library, and NHTSA. The current release does not use Clerk or a Supabase app backend.

Sensitive code contents

The app does not intentionally send decoded QR or barcode contents to advertising or analytics services by default. Sensitive values such as Wi-Fi passwords are redacted from exports and summaries where the app identifies them as sensitive. Avoid scanning or sharing secrets unless you trust the destination.

Retention

Local app data remains until you delete it, reset the app, or remove the app. We do not maintain a server-side user-account or scan-history database for this release. Apple, RevenueCat, and optional third-party information services retain information according to their own policies and legal obligations.

Your choices

Reset local data from Settings → Developer → Reset everything & onboarding. Manage permissions in iOS Settings. Cancel Premium in Apple ID → Subscriptions. Use Restore Purchases after reinstalling. There is no app account to delete in the current release. Privacy questions can be sent to acceptus.trust@riseup.net.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 or the minimum age required in your country.

International users

Service providers may process data in the United States or other countries where they operate.

Changes

We may update this policy. The Effective date will change when we do.
